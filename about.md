## About This Publication

*Manuscript Theme* is a Jekyll theme designed for writers, essayists, and independent publishers who want their blog to feel like a magazine rather than a feed.

### Design Philosophy

Print typography has centuries of accumulated wisdom about making text readable. Manuscript Theme borrows from that tradition:

- **Cormorant Garamond** display font for headlines — high-contrast, elegant, with true italic
- **Lora** for body text — the best web-optimized serif for long-form reading
- **Drop caps** on first paragraphs — the classic editorial marker for a new essay
- **Pull quotes** styled as oversized magazine callouts
- **Warm cream background** (`#faf7f2`) — easier on the eyes than pure white
- **Oxford double rule** (`3px double`) as a structural separator
